EVM Storage & Assembly Optimization

In EVM, dynamic state storage uses 256-bit (32-byte) key-value slots. Standard mapping dynamic keys are calculated via:

Slot = keccak256(abi.encode(key, baseSlot))

Note on Padding: EVM uses Keccak-256 with 0x01 padding byte (distinct from standard SHA3-256 0x06 byte).

• Cold SLOAD: 2,100 Gas
• New SSTORE: 20,000 Gas
• Scratch Memory (0x00-0x3f): Used via Huff/Yul assembly macros to compute hashes without memory expansion fees.
Struct packing ensures variables like uint8 and bool share a single 32-byte slot, drastically reducing secondary market execution fees.

⚖ Regulatory Frameworks, Legal Abstractions, & CIS Risk Mitigation

Navigating domestic Indian tokenization requires overcoming rigid statutory hurdles: SEBI Collective Investment Scheme (CIS) classification, Transfer of Property Act (1882) constraints, PMLA compliance, and Virtual Digital Asset (VDA) tax exposure.

SEBI CIS Doctrine (Section 11AA)

High Regulatory Risk

Under Section 11AA of the SEBI Act (1992), any arrangement pooling investor funds exceeding ₹100 Crores where investors lack day-to-day managerial control constitutes a Collective Investment Scheme (CIS). Unregistered CIS operations trigger severe criminal liability under Section 24.

4-Part SEBI CIS Test:
1. Payments are pooled for scheme profit.
2. Investors receive rental/capital income.
3. Asset managed on behalf of investors.
4. Investors possess zero operational day-to-day control.

Property Law & SPV Abstraction

Legal Solution

Under the Transfer of Property Act (1882) & Registration Act (1908), direct blockchain token transfers hold ZERO legal validity for property title transfer in India and require physical stamp duty execution.

The SPV Legal Safe Harbor:
1. Land is deeded directly to an SPV (Pvt Ltd / LLP).
2. SPV issues corporate debentures/shares to investors.
3. Tokens represent fractional security claims in SPV equity.
4. Complies with Companies Act (2013) & RERA (2016).
